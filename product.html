Our Services

Sparkline Electrical Services offers a full range of electrical work for homes and small businesses, carried out by registered, insured electricians.

From full house rewires to adding new plug points and lighting circuits, we handle residential wiring projects of any size, with all work certified to South African standards.

Commercial Installations

We install and upgrade electrical systems for small offices, shops, and workshops, including distribution boards, lighting, and power circuits designed for commercial use.

Fault Finding & Maintenance

Tripping breakers, flickering lights, or an unexplained power outage? Our team diagnoses and repairs electrical faults quickly, and offers ongoing maintenance plans to prevent future issues.
